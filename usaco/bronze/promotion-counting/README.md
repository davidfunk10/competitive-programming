## Promotion Counting (USACO Bronze)

For this ad hoc problem, I started out by realizing that the difference between platinum final and platinum initial members is equal to the amount of people that promoted from gold to platinum. I initially thought that you just take the difference between gold final, gold initial, silver final, and silver intial to get the promotions from silver to gold and bronze to silver, respectively. However, I soon came to find out that that does not work because some people promote across 2 ranks at once. I played around with the numbers until I came up with a formula to find the correct promotion numbers, considering the people that pass through mutiple ranks in this one contest. Basically, how the formula works is you just add the difference of the rank above to the difference in the final and initial values of the rank below, and you will end up with the amount of people who transferred from the rank below the lower rank into the lower rank.
